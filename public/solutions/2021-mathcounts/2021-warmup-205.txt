Step 1: Set up the equation
Let $e$ represent the measure of an exterior angle.
The complement of the exterior angle is $90^\circ - e$.
The supplement of the exterior angle is $180^\circ - e$.

According to the problem, the complement is equal to $\frac{1}{4}$ of the supplement:
$90^\circ - e = \frac{1}{4}(180^\circ - e)$

Step 2: Solve for the exterior angle ($e$)
Multiply both sides by 4 to clear the fraction:
$4(90^\circ - e) = 180^\circ - e$
$360^\circ - 4e = 180^\circ - e$

Rearrange the terms to solve for $e$:
$360^\circ - 180^\circ = 4e - e$
$180^\circ = 3e$
$e = 60^\circ$

Step 3: Find the number of sides ($n$)
For any regular polygon, the sum of all exterior angles is always $360^\circ$, and each exterior angle is given by $\frac{360^\circ}{n}$, where $n$ is the number of sides:
$60^\circ = \frac{360^\circ}{n}$

Solving for $n$:
$n = \frac{360}{60} = 6$

The polygon has 6 sides (it is a regular hexagon)
