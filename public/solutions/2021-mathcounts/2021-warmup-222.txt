HINT:  0 is a multiple of 3.

Calculate the Total Outcomes
Each of the 9 coins has 2 possible outcomes (Heads or Tails), making the total number of possible outcomes:
$2^9 = 512$

Identify the Favorable Outcomes
The number of heads $k$ that are multiples of 3 are $k \in \{0, 3, 6, 9\}$.

Use the combinations formula $\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$ to find the number of ways to flip exactly $k$ heads:
0 heads: $\binom{9}{0} = 1$
3 heads: $\binom{9}{3} = 84$
6 heads: $\binom{9}{6} = 84$
9 heads: $\binom{9}{9} = 1$

Sum the Favorable Outcomes
$1 + 84 + 84 + 1 = 170$

$\text{Probability} = \frac{170}{512} = \frac{85}{256}$
