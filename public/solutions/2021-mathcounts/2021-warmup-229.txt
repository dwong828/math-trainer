Set Up the Equations with Unit Conversions
Let $s$ be the side length of the cube in feet.
Volume in $\text{ft}^3$: $V = s^3 = N$
Side length in inches: $12s \text{ inches}$
Surface Area in $\text{in}^2$: $A = 6(12s)^2 = 6 \times 144s^2 = 864s^2 = N$

Solve for the Side Length $s$
Equate the two expressions for $N$:
$s^3 = 864s^2$
$s = 864 \text{ feet}$

Find the Value of $N$
Substitute $s = 864$ into the volume equation:
$N = s^3 = (864)^3$

Determine Prime Factorization of $N$
First, find the prime factorization of $864$:
$864 = 2^5 \times 3^3$

Now compute $N = (864)^3$:
$N = (2^5 \times 3^3)^3 = 2^{15} \times 3^9$

We get $a = 15$ and $b = 9$
$a + b = 15 + 9 = 24$
