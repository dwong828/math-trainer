Understand the Geometric Sequence Formula
In a geometric sequence with first term $t_1$ and common ratio $r$, the $n^{\text{th}}$ term is given by:
$t_n = t_1 \cdot r^{n-1}$

Find the Common Ratio $r$
The terms of the sequence are:
$t_1 = 3$
$t_2 = a$
$t_3 = b$
$t_4 = 375$

Using the formula for the fourth term $t_4$:
$375 = 3 \cdot r^3$

Divide both sides by $3$:
$r^3 = 125$
$r = 5$

Calculate $a$ and $b$
$a = t_2 = 3 \cdot 5 = 15$
$b = t_3 = 15 \cdot 5 = 75$

$a + b = 15 + 75 = 90$
